### Short description

Bud n Spud Creator TV is a creator-led scene workbench for building repeatable character animation workflows. A creator starts with a story, three visual references and two synthetic voice auditions, shapes an editable prompt, and carries a ComfyUI graph into a compatible generation environment. Hermes can assist with planning and operating the workflow; the creator remains responsible for reviewing and executing it.

### What makes it useful

The kit makes the ingredients and production history inspectable: starter references, actual historical API graphs, prompts, provenance and model-rights notes live together. It gives creators a starting point for another episode without presenting generated media as deterministic or the prototype as a finished autonomous station.
